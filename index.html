About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at {{ a.school }}, where I work in the {{ a.lab }} with {{ a.advisor }}. I expect to graduate in 2027.

My research is on hallucinations in generative AI: cases where a model produces output that sounds correct but is false or not supported by its input. I study this problem in three settings: language models that generate text, vision-language models that describe images, and audio-language models that process speech. I develop methods to detect and correct these errors, and I test how models fail under adversarial prompts. My applications include transportation safety and health care.

I am looking for full-time research positions starting in 2027.
